Buying Property in North Cyprus: A Practical Guide for International Buyers

Buying a property in the Turkish Republic of Northern Cyprus (TRNC) is a straightforward process for international purchasers, provided the applicable purchase limits, permissions and registration requirements are followed.

Whether you are looking for a permanent home, a holiday property or an investment, this guide summarises the key points international buyers should know.

A Simple Example of the Buying Process

For most purchasers, the process can be summarised as follows:

Step 1 — Choose your property

Select the apartment, villa or house that suits your requirements.

Step 2 — Legal review

Your lawyer reviews the property's legal and title documentation and the sales agreement.

Step 3 — Sign the sales agreement

The agreement sets out:

  • Purchase price
  • Payment schedule
  • Completion arrangements
  • Taxes and fees
  • Delivery date
  • Title-transfer arrangements

Step 4 — Register the contract

The sales contract is registered with the relevant Land Registry where required.

Step 5 — Apply for purchase permission

The relevant application is submitted to the Council of Ministers.

Step 6 — Complete the financial and registration requirements

Applicable taxes, fees and charges are settled.

Step 7 — Title transfer

The property is transferred into the purchaser's name when the relevant conditions have been completed.

Step 8 — Residence application

If you intend to live in North Cyprus, you can apply for the appropriate residence permit.

1. Who Can Buy Property in North Cyprus?

Non-TRNC citizens can purchase property in North Cyprus, subject to the limits and permissions set by the applicable property legislation.

The current framework provides different acquisition limits depending on the buyer's nationality.

General international buyers

For buyers who fall within the general foreign-national category, the current framework allows, subject to the relevant conditions:

  • Up to 3 apartments
  • Up to 2 detached/two-storey villas in the relevant development category
  • A plot of up to 1,338 m² for the construction of one residence
  • A detached residence on land of up to 3,300 m², subject to the applicable conditions

Citizens of Turkey / reciprocal-category countries

Citizens of countries recognised under the relevant reciprocal provisions receive higher acquisition limits. In practice, this is particularly relevant to Turkish citizens.

The current framework provides for:

  • Up to 6 apartments
  • Up to 3 detached/two-storey villas in the relevant development category

The exact entitlement should always be confirmed for the purchaser and property at the time of purchase, as legislation can be updated.

The acquisition limits apply to the purchaser's overall property ownership and should therefore be considered before purchasing multiple units.

2. Is Government Permission Required?

Yes.

International buyers are required to obtain the relevant permission to purchase property from the Council of Ministers in Northern Cyprus.

This is a standard part of the purchasing process and is normally handled with the assistance of the purchaser's legal representative or developer, as it's a straightforward online application.

A typical purchase process is:

Property selection → Legal checks → Sales agreement or Deed → Contract registration (if sales agreement) → Purchase permission application → Completion of transfer

The Council of Ministers regularly approves applications from international purchasers, subject to the property being legally available for acquisition.

Your lawyer will normally guide you through the documentation and application process.

3. What Additional Costs Should Buyers Budget For?

Depending on the property, seller, transaction structure and purchaser's circumstances, additional costs can include:

VAT

VAT applies to new-build/professional developments. The rate payable should be confirmed before signing the contract; it currently stands at 5%.

Stamp Duty / Transfer Fee

For international buyers, the current transfer fee is 12%, generally payable in two stages: 6% when the sales contract is registered and the remaining 6% upon transfer of title. Stamp duty is also payable on the sales agreement. The exact amount of taxes and fees depends on the property, transaction structure and applicable legislation at the time of purchase.

For citizens of Turkey, the fee is 6% for a first property purchase, generally payable in two stages: 3% when the sales contract is registered and the remaining 3% upon transfer of title.

Other possible costs

Depending on the development, additional costs may include:

  • Legal / solicitor fees
  • Land Registry fees
  • Electricity connection and meter charges
  • Water connection and meter charges
  • Transformer / infrastructure contribution
  • Municipality-related charges
  • Site management / maintenance fees
  • Bank and transfer charges, where applicable

Important: These costs vary by property and transaction. Buyers should request a complete “total acquisition cost” statement before signing.

4. Legal Fees

Using an independent lawyer is strongly recommended for international buyers.

Your lawyer can assist with:

  • Reviewing the title/ownership documents
  • Checking the property's legal status
  • Reviewing the sales agreement
  • Registering the sales contract
  • Applying for purchase permission
  • Advising on taxes and fees
  • Completing the title transfer

For new developments, the developer's sales team can normally provide the expected standard costs, while your independent lawyer can confirm the final figures for your individual purchase.

5. Can Property Owners Live in North Cyprus?

Yes.

Property ownership can provide a route to applying for a short-term residence permit.

The TRNC Immigration Department specifically provides a residence-permit category for foreigners who own property in North Cyprus. The property must be suitable for residential occupation and used as a residence.

What if the title deed has not yet been transferred?

You may still be able to apply for residence based on your property purchase.

Under the current rules, where the property has been purchased under a sales contract:

  • At least one-third of the purchase price must have been paid, and
  • An application must have been made for the relevant Council of Ministers purchase permission.

This can allow purchasers of new-build properties to establish their residence arrangements before final title transfer.

7. Does Buying Property Give Citizenship?

No.

Buying a property does not automatically grant TRNC citizenship.

Property ownership and residence are separate from citizenship.

However, property ownership can provide an attractive route for international purchasers who wish to spend extended periods of time living in North Cyprus.

8. Can I Bring My Car From My Home Country?

Yes, in certain circumstances.

North Cyprus has specific rules for people moving to the country and bringing their personally owned home furniture and vehicle with them.

Under the applicable vehicle-import regulations, a person coming to settle in North Cyprus may be able to import a vehicle registered in their own name, subject to the relevant conditions and import procedures. Because vehicle-import regulations can change, buyers planning to bring a vehicle should confirm the current requirements with the Trade Department before shipping the vehicle.

The Trade Department specifically states that individuals importing a vehicle for personal use should make the relevant application themselves.

9. Can I Buy a Car in North Cyprus?

Yes.

International residents can purchase vehicles locally in North Cyprus, subject to the normal vehicle registration, insurance, road tax and licensing requirements.

For many new residents, purchasing a vehicle locally can be the simplest option, particularly where importing an existing vehicle would involve additional customs and registration procedures.

10. Can I Drive With My Existing Driving Licence?

This depends on your nationality and the status of your licence.

International visitors can generally use a valid foreign driving licence for the period permitted under the applicable rules.

For people who become residents, the requirements can be different, and a local driving licence may eventually be required.

Turkish driving licences

There is a specific reciprocal recognition and exchange arrangement between the Republic of Türkiye and the TRNC.

The arrangement was updated in 2025 and formally published in 2026, providing for the reciprocal recognition and exchange of national driving licences.

11. Driving in North Cyprus

North Cyprus drives on the left side of the road.

This is an important point for buyers coming from countries where driving is on the right.

A valid driving licence, vehicle registration, insurance and road tax are required when driving locally.

Before You Purchase

A professional developer should provide buyers with a clear summary of:

Purchase price + VAT (if applicable) + transfer/tax costs + stamp duty + legal fees + infrastructure/connection charges + any development-specific costs

This allows buyers to understand their total acquisition cost from the beginning.

As tax, registration and immigration rules can be amended from time to time, the final figures and requirements should always be confirmed for the individual property and purchaser at the time of purchase.

This article is provided for general information only and does not constitute legal, tax or immigration advice. Rules and rates can change — always confirm current requirements with your lawyer and the relevant authorities before making a purchase decision.

Why Should You Buy in Famagusta, Northern Cyprus

Your Mediterranean home. Your investment. Your lifestyle.

Famagusta offers international buyers an exciting opportunity to own a home in the Mediterranean while benefiting from an attractive and developing property market.

With its beautiful coastline, historic character, vibrant university community and growing international appeal, Famagusta combines the lifestyle of a Mediterranean destination with the advantages of a year-round city that offers greater investment potential than any other part of Northern Cyprus.

Discover Your Famagusta Home

Whether you are looking for a permanent residence, a Mediterranean second home or a property investment, we invite you to discover our latest apartments in Famagusta.

A Mediterranean Lifestyle Within Reach

Imagine having your own apartment in the Mediterranean — somewhere you can escape to for holidays, enjoy extended stays or eventually make your permanent home.

Famagusta offers:

  • Beautiful beaches and coastline, among the cleanest on the island
  • A warm Mediterranean climate
  • Historic Old City and cultural attractions
  • Restaurants, cafés and local amenities
  • An established international university community
  • A relaxed Mediterranean lifestyle
  • Modern residential developments
  • An increasingly international population

And compared with many established Mediterranean property markets, Northern Cyprus remains relatively accessible to international buyers.

Recognised by Forbes

Northern Cyprus has attracted international attention for its combination of affordability and Mediterranean lifestyle.

Forbes has highlighted Northern Cyprus among affordable overseas property markets, pointing to its comparatively accessible property prices and developing tourism and infrastructure. Forbes has also identified the region's tourism growth, including new hotels, marinas and other major developments, as factors attracting international property investors.

Why Famagusta?

A Real City, Not Just a Holiday Resort

One of Famagusta's greatest advantages is that it has a genuine year-round population.

The city is home to Eastern Mediterranean University (EMU), an internationally recognised university with students from all over the world.

This creates established demand for accommodation throughout the academic year, alongside demand from local residents, professionals and international visitors.

For property owners, this creates flexibility: your apartment can be enjoyed as a personal residence, holiday home or potentially rented to students, professionals or other tenants, depending on your chosen strategy.

A Property With More Than One Purpose

Your apartment can adapt to your lifestyle.

  • Live here — Enjoy Famagusta as your Mediterranean home or second residence.
  • Holiday here — Keep your own private base for holidays and extended stays.
  • Rent it — Depending on the property and local regulations, generate rental income when you are not using it.
  • Keep it for the future — Build a long-term property asset in an emerging Mediterranean market.

No investment can guarantee future rental income or capital appreciation, but choosing a property with multiple potential uses can provide greater flexibility.

An Emerging Property Market

Northern Cyprus is still developing as an international residential and tourism destination.

Continued investment in:

  • Tourism
  • Hotels
  • Universities
  • Infrastructure
  • Marina and leisure facilities
  • Residential developments
  • International services

is contributing to the region's growing international profile.

For investors, the attraction is the opportunity to participate in a developing market rather than paying the established-market prices found in many traditional Mediterranean destinations.

A City With Year-Round Rental Potential

Famagusta's combination of university life, local employment, tourism and international residents creates several potential rental markets.

Depending on the location and property type, owners may consider:

  • Student accommodation
  • Long-term residential rental
  • Professional accommodation
  • Holiday/short-term rental, where permitted

This diversity can make Famagusta particularly attractive to buyers who want more than a purely seasonal holiday property.

Make North Cyprus Your Home

International property owners may be eligible to apply for a short-term residence permit, subject to the applicable immigration requirements.

This means your Famagusta apartment can be more than a holiday destination — it can become the foundation for spending much more of your life in North Cyprus.

Whether you are planning retirement, a second home, extended Mediterranean living or simply more freedom to spend time abroad, property ownership can provide a practical route towards establishing your life here.

More Than a Property Purchase

For many international buyers, the appeal of Famagusta is that it combines several things in one decision:

A Mediterranean lifestyle. A home of your own. A potential rental property. An international community. And an opportunity to participate in a developing property market.

You don't have to choose between a holiday home and an investment. With the right property, you can potentially have both.
